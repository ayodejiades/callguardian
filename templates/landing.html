How it works

Bring your list

Accounts in the dashboard, or rows in any Google Sheet: name, phone, timezone, consent, rule pack.

CallGuardian checks it

Consent, call-window, suppression, and attempt-cap rules run before anything dials. CLEARED, or BLOCKED with the exact reason.

CALL-E places the call

Only cleared accounts get dialed. The outcome and a tamper-evident audit hash land back in the row.

Gate, not a wrapper

Every dial is checked against consent, call-window, suppression, and attempt-cap rules before CALL-E is ever asked to place it. Reasons are named, never a bare pass/fail.

Tamper-evident audit log

Every decision and call outcome is written to a hash-chained log. Edit one line after the fact and verification catches exactly where the chain breaks.

One engine, pluggable rule packs

A fifth vertical is a new rule pack, not new engine code.
